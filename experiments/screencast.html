Cursor-free capture prototype (chrome.debugger · Page.startScreencast)

Pick a tab, press Start: the tab comes to the front and frames are captured for the chosen time. Move the mouse over the page, hover links and buttons, and click a few things, then come back here. The results show whether the frames contain the system cursor, the real frame rate and the gaps between frames. Chrome shows a "started debugging this browser" bar while it runs — that is expected.
